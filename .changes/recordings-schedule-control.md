Scheduled recordings now report their outcome — scheduled, recording, canceled, or failed with the reason — in the recordings manager, and a pending one can be rescheduled instead of only canceled. The Windows build ships a tray icon again, so closing the window keeps the app and its schedules alive, and a notification confirms when a scheduled recording starts or fails.
