highlight: Scheduled recordings and a tray-resident app
---

Channels offer "Schedule recording…" in the right-click menu (M3U, Xtream and Stalker), IPTVnator keeps running in the tray so a scheduled recording still starts with the window closed, and a Recordings entry in the rail opens past recordings. Stalker links expire, so a Stalker schedule gets a fresh link from the portal right before it starts.
